Transparent 4-Step Process

How AgriSure Protects Farmers & Buyers

From initial crop listing to final delivery and payment settlement, every milestone is structured, verified, and legally documented.

1

List or Discover Crops

Farmers post upcoming harvest details with expected pricing and quantity. Buyers filter listings by location, quality grade, and harvest timeline.

2

Two-Way Negotiation

Parties exchange transparent counter-offers, agree on quality specifications, delivery conditions, and fair contract rates without middlemen.

3

Digital Acceptance & Escrow

Formal bilingual PDF contract is generated. Both parties accept the terms digitally, and the buyer deposits advance into secured escrow.

4

Delivery & Guaranteed Payout

Track harvest progress and transportation. Upon buyer delivery verification, funds are instantly transferred directly to the farmer's bank.
