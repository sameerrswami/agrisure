How Contract Farming Works on AgriSure

A step-by-step guide for farmers and buyers to establish reliable, legally compliant agricultural supply contracts.

1

Farmer Lists Produce

The farmer publishes details of the expected crop: variety, quantity (in Quintals/Tonnes), quality standards, estimated harvest date, and proposed price.

2

Buyer Submits Request or Negotiation Offer

Buyers explore listings on the marketplace. They can request immediate contract terms or start a two-way price negotiation with counter-offers.

3

Bilingual Contract Generation & Digital Acceptance

Once agreement is reached, AgriSure compiles the official PDF contract. Both parties review and accept the exact contract version digitally.

4

Harvest Tracking, Delivery & Payout

Production milestones are logged. When harvested and delivered per agreed specifications, funds from escrow are disbursed to the farmer.
